## Why the reference is strong

The reference joins a human truth to one specific high-stakes consequence and a contradictory institutional verdict:

1. relationship/truth A;
2. a concrete high-stakes situation;
3. exclusion or consequence B;
4. an independently meaningful banner that closes the contradiction.

Its power comes from specificity. `延命治療の相談に入れなかった` names exactly what the protagonist was excluded from. A shorter generic phrase such as `扉の外に残された` may be accurate but loses the event and its stakes.

## Generalizable content rules

1. Read the current TITLE first and list what it already reveals.
2. Read only the current approved DESCRIPTION/SCRIPT and find the strongest accurate contradiction, discovery/shock and emotional conflict/secret.
3. Before rendering, write at least three independent directions:
   - A: `contradiction`
   - B: `discovery_shock`
   - C: `emotional_conflict_secret`
4. Score every direction from 1–5 on:
   - story accuracy;
   - specificity;
   - stakes;
   - curiosity;
   - title complement;
   - Japanese naturalness;
   - 1–2 second clarity.
5. The selected winner must have the highest total and the rendered rows/banner must match it exactly.
6. Never weaken a concrete high-stakes fact merely to shorten text or enlarge the font. Solve length with line breaks, semantic role, safe-space use and typography search.
7. Thumbnail and title must complement one another. Do not spend thumbnail space repeating the title's main reveal.
8. Do not reveal the final payoff/resolution merely to make the hook stronger.
9. Japanese must be natural for an older Japanese audience, specific without becoming administrative or machine-translated.
10. Every proposed Japanese line must be reviewed with a Vietnamese meaning.

## Semantic typography rules

- `context`: establishes relationship or situation.
- `trigger`: the concrete event or attention detail.
- `hook` / `payoff`: the consequence, shock or contradiction.
- `banner`: an independent thesis or emotional/legal verdict—not decoration.

Visual hierarchy follows meaning, not text length. A short secondary line must not become largest merely because auto-fit allows it. Main rows must remain large and bold at 360 px. FACE is hard-protected; BODY/HAND/PROP are conditional and may be overlapped if storytelling remains intact.

Forbidden generic banners include:

- `衝撃の真実` → Sự thật gây sốc
- `まさかの結末` → Kết cục không ngờ
- `その理由とは？` → Lý do là gì?

## Scene and subject rules

- Use a scene that adds current-story evidence, not a decorative portrait background.
- Subject emotion must match the current scene and stakes.
- A clean/darker typography zone is useful, but never copy this reference's scene or composition to an unrelated episode.
- Keep the face large and readable. Protect FACE, not unnecessary empty space.

## Required post-render challenge

Do not pass solely because nothing overlaps. Ask:

1. Can any row become materially larger while FACE and story remain safe?
2. Is there wasted safe space?
3. Is any row visibly small, thin or weak?
4. Does visual hierarchy match semantic hierarchy?
5. Are BODY/HAND/PROP overprotected?
6. Does the 360 px version still hit in 1–2 seconds?
7. Is visual authority materially weaker than this quality reference?

If a meaningful improvement remains, revise and render again before human review.
